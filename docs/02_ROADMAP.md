# Roadmap

Strategic direction for theroberthu.com as an editorial AI Commerce publication.
This is about initiatives, not individual coding tasks (those live in
[03_WEBSITE_BACKLOG.md](03_WEBSITE_BACKLOG.md)).

Horizons:
- **Now** - in progress or next up.
- **Next** - committed, not yet started.
- **Later** - directionally agreed, not scheduled.
- **Future** - aspirational, revisit as the field matures.

## Now

- **Finish the editorial repositioning.** Consulting is retired; close the
  remaining gaps (flagship standardization, stale docs, dormant lead
  infrastructure) so the site reads as a coherent publication end to end.
- **Standardize the flagship pages.** Bring `/geo` and `/aeo` to one shared
  standard (ending label, "In Short" primer, `Article` schema).
- **Strengthen internal linking and topic clusters.** Tighten the GEO and AEO
  clusters and cross-link the blog into both.

## Next

- **AI Commerce hub.** A top-level `/ai-commerce` flagship that frames the whole
  domain and routes into GEO, AEO, and the platform clusters. Currently AI
  Commerce is a theme without a home page.
- **Entity map / structured knowledge graph.** Deliberate entity linking
  (platforms, concepts, people) across pages and JSON-LD so AI systems resolve
  the site as an authority on AI Commerce.
- **Platform coverage clusters.** Give the major agents (Amazon Alexa for
  Shopping, Walmart Sparky, Google AI Mode) durable cluster pages, mirroring the
  GEO subpage model.

## Later

- **Research hub.** A home for RecoScope-backed data and original benchmarks,
  presented as ongoing research rather than one-off posts.
- **Glossary of AI Commerce terms.** An evergreen, linkable reference that
  reinforces official terminology and earns definitional citations.
- **Convert the archived `/services/*` pages** into genuine research hubs, or
  retire them fully, per the Archive and Evolve decision.

## Future

- **Annual "State of AI Commerce" report.** A flagship, citable, linkable
  yearly synthesis.
- **Interactive visuals and data tools.** Let readers explore recommendation
  data directly.
- **Recognized reference status.** The point where operators, executives, and AI
  systems cite theroberthu.com by default when explaining how AI is changing
  commerce.
