## REVISIONS (Editorial Board, applied before publication)

1. "Every step leaves a record on a surface somebody owns, and the click is what
   stitches those records into a sequence."
   -> "Many of the most important steps left records on surfaces somebody could
   instrument, and the click often stitched those records into a usable sequence."
   Reason: the original overstated how complete pre-AI observability ever was.

2. "OpenAI and Google know what was asked, what was ranked, and why it was ranked
   that way. A third-party stack, however good, is inferring from outside the
   system."
   -> "OpenAI and Google can observe the prompt or query and what their systems
   returned. Third parties cannot see the full decision process behind those
   outputs."
   Reason: the original attributed to the platforms a complete self-knowledge of
   their own ranking rationale that is not established. The following sentences
   about joining datasets widening the observable surface without establishing
   causality or incrementality were preserved verbatim.

3. "If it works, the useful unit stops being the visit and becomes something
   closer to the appearance. Whether the product got onto the machine's shortlist
   at all."
   -> "If it works, the useful unit expands beyond the visit to include something
   closer to the appearance: whether the product entered the machine's
   consideration set at all."
   Reason: "stops being" implied replacement rather than addition.

## EVIDENCE NOTES

**The single most load-bearing fact.** NIQ's FAQ asks "Is Agentic Commerce
Measurement available today?" and answers "No. NIQ is currently building the
solution." Everything in the article is constrained by that. Do not let a future
revision describe this as a shipped capability.

**Roadmap vs capability.** Initial version expected Q4 2026, "beginning with a
focused set of categories and markets." Markets not announced. Planned platform
coverage: ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Claude.

**Contribution split, verbatim.** NIQ: "product intelligence, consumer behavior
data and retail sales measurement." Similarweb: "the foundational digital data,
adding visibility into AI-driven consumer behavior inside generative AI platforms
and along the agentic path to purchase."

**Methodology is undisclosed for the joint product.** Neither release explains how
it will work. The article says so explicitly. The panel detail cited in the piece
("drawn from real user panel conversations, not synthetic prompts") comes from
Similarweb's SEPARATE 2026-08-17 AI Ads release and describes THAT dataset. The
article flags this scoping limit in its own text. Do not let anyone "tighten" the
piece by attributing the panel method to the joint product.

**Similarweb self-describes its outputs as estimates.** Standard disclaimer:
"estimated metrics and digital insights generated by Similarweb using its
proprietary algorithms." Used to support the sample-not-census point without
disparaging the product, since it is the company's own language.

**The 75% / 20% gap is real and is NIQ's own.** The 2026-08-27 release states both
"Nearly 75% of shoppers use AI during product discovery" and "20% of consumers
already use AI as part of the shopping process." Neither is defined, no
methodology, sample, or geography is given. The article presents this as a caution
about materiality, not as a contradiction.

**Deliberately not claimed.** That the collaboration solves attribution; that
either company can see private prompts; that AI visibility causes sales; that
traditional analytics is obsolete. The strongest claim in the piece is that the
collaboration is "an attempt to instrument the consideration set," labelled as
Robert's interpretation.

**Known echo, accepted.** "Consideration set" appears four times, including in
both the interpretation section (revision 3) and the closing paragraph. Read as a
deliberate reprise. Flagged to the Board at publication.

**Minor source oddity, deliberately excluded.** NIQ's forward-looking-statements
boilerplate on this release refers to "ConnectAI Charter Program Clients" rather
than to this solution, and appears copy-pasted from a prior release. Immaterial
and would read as a cheap shot.

**Overlap.** 80 published posts scanned at draft time. Similarweb, NIQ, NielsenIQ,
Nielsen: zero prior mentions. "Zero-click" and "consideration set": zero. Genuinely
new coverage.
