## NON-NEGOTIABLE: THE H1 IS A DESIGN BOUNDARY, NOT SETTLED POLICY

The H1 states a boundary strongly. Four body guardrails carry the qualification,
and ALL of them must survive any future edit:

1. Choudhary's full title is given: "non-executive chairman and independent
   director". He is not an executive setting policy.
2. "Here is my own reading rather than anything NPCI claimed" flags the
   primitive section as Robert's interpretation.
3. "NPCI has published no specification. Choudhary described examining
   protocols, which is a live process rather than a shipped design."
4. "A keynote is not policy... the distance between that and a published
   specification with a rollout date is considerable."

If any of those are removed, the H1 becomes an overclaim. They are the reason it
can stand.

## REVISIONS (Editorial Board, applied before publication)

1. "An AI agent is probabilistic by construction. Give it the same situation
   twice and it may reason differently..."
   -> "LLM-based agent reasoning can be probabilistic. The same situation can
   produce different interpretations, and that flexibility is part of what makes
   these systems useful for understanding what someone actually wants."
   Reason: the original overstated probabilism as an inherent property of all
   agents. The deterministic/auditable payment contrast that follows was
   preserved verbatim.

2. "That is most of the architecture an agent needs."
   -> "That gives UPI an existing primitive that maps unusually well onto the
   agentic-payment problem."
   Reason: the original implied UPI already has a complete agentic-payment
   architecture. It does not. The mandate/agent/rail sequence that follows was
   preserved verbatim.
