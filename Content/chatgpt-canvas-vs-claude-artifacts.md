ChatGPT Canvas: The Content & Code Money Machine
What Makes Canvas Special
Canvas gives you a collaborative workspace where you can edit code and documents alongside ChatGPT in real-time. Think Google Docs but with an AI assistant that can actually write, debug, and improve your work. This is particularly powerful for small business owners looking to scale their operations. For those interested in content generation, you might also want to explore HeyGen for AI video creation.

• Real-time collaborative editing

Canvas Limitations

The Verdict: Which Tool Should You Choose?
My Recommendation:
Choose ChatGPT Canvas if: You're focused on content creation, web development, or need real-time collaboration with clients. It's perfect for scaling content agencies and freelance coding work.

Choose Claude Artifacts if: You want to offer high-value business consulting, strategy work, or data analysis. The interactive artifacts justify higher project rates and longer-term retainers.

Choose Both if: You have the budget ($40/month) and want to dominate multiple revenue streams. Many successful AI entrepreneurs use Canvas for day-to-day client work and Artifacts for premium strategy projects.

Pro Tip: Start with whichever tool matches your current skills better, then add the second tool once you're generating $2K+/month with the first. This way you're not overwhelming yourself while learning.
