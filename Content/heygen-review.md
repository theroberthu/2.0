What is HeyGen?
HeyGen is an AI video generation platform that creates professional videos using AI avatars, voice synthesis, and automated editing. Think of it as having a video production studio in your browser - no camera, no crew, no scheduling hassles.

Recognized as G2's #1 Fastest Growing Product of 2025, HeyGen powers over 100,000 businesses. The platform tackles the three biggest pain points in video creation:

Camera anxiety: Not everyone wants to be the face of their brand - AI avatars solve this
Production costs: Traditional video shoots cost $1,500-5,000+ per finished minute
Global reach: One video becomes 40+ localized versions with lip-sync translation

The Translation Feature is a Game-Changer

For businesses expanding globally, this feature alone justifies the subscription. Traditionally, localizing a single video for 5 markets meant 5 separate productions, voice actors, and editing sessions - easily $10,000+ per video.

With HeyGen, you create once and translate instantly. The lip-sync technology ensures your avatar's mouth movements match the new language naturally. It's not perfect (trained eyes can spot minor inconsistencies), but it's 90% there and improving rapidly.

How to Get Started (The Smart Way)
Don't jump straight into paid plans. Start with HeyGen's free tier - 3 videos per month is enough to test your actual use case.

1
Start Small
Create one short video in your niche. Test how your audience responds to AI-generated content before committing.

2
Pick Your Avatar Wisely
Choose less popular avatars or invest in a custom one if building a brand. Some stock avatars are overused.

3
Script for AI
Write conversational, shorter sentences. AI avatars work better with natural speech patterns - avoid jargon and complex phrasing.

4
Test Translation Early
If global reach is your goal, test the translation feature before scaling. Some languages perform better than others.
